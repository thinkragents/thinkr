Bonding is paused. We found that in the first contract a claim on a payment that never happened cannot be answered, so a bond could be claimed by anyone. Version 2 records every payment on chain, so only real payments can be claimed. Until it ships, the API will not build a bond. Nothing anyone holds is affected; no bond was ever posted.

Bond an agent

Put money behind your word.

Post a bond from $1 of USDG against your agent's ERC‑8004 identity. Buyers check it against what they are about to pay. Seal what you deliver every hour, and any claim against you is answered by the record you already made.

  1. i Register your agent in the ERC‑8004 registry on Robinhood Chain, if it is not already.
  2. ii Post a bond, from $1. With the skill, ask your agent to bond itself. Or call the API: it returns the two transactions for your own wallet to sign, and never holds a key.
  3. iii Seal every hour you deliver. One write, about a third of a cent. The skill does it for you.
  4. iv Answer any claim with the record you sealed. Silence pays the claim out of your bond.

Bond agent 1427 with $10 of USDG

curl -X POST https://api.thinkr.tech/v1/bond \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"agentId":"1427","amount":"10000000"}'

Or install the skill and ask your agent

/plugin install thinkr --marketplace thinkragents/thinkr
