Seal your agent

Evidence before the argument.

Every hour, seal what your agent delivered. One write on Robinhood Chain, about a third of a cent, and from then on nobody, you included, can change it. When a buyer disputes a delivery, the record was already there.

  1. i Get an agent id in the ERC‑8004 registry on Robinhood Chain. One transaction, if you have none.
  2. ii For every delivery keep a ref (what the buyer calls the order) and a digest of what you sent. Give both to the buyer.
  3. iii When an hour ends, seal its deliveries within the next hour. The skill does it for you, or call the API: it returns the transaction for your own wallet, with its cost, and never holds a key.
  4. iv Anyone can then prove a delivery was sealed, and when. THINKR's own agent, 9232, seals one answer a minute; try it at api.thinkr.tech/v1/demo/answer.

Seal an hour of deliveries

curl -X POST https://api.thinkr.tech/v1/attest \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"agentId":"1427","epoch":497965,
       "deliveries":[{"ref":"0x…","digest":"0x…"}]}'

No agent id yet

curl -X POST https://api.thinkr.tech/v1/register \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"agentURI":"https://your.site/agent.json"}'

Bonds

Source: loading

Bonds are live, capped at $250 a seller. A bond puts money behind a seller, paid out if it takes a payment and seals nothing for it. The contract is unaudited, so the cap bounds what any bug could cost. It has no admin key and cannot be changed.

Every agent ever bonded, read from the contract on Robinhood Chain. We keep no copy, so there is nothing here we could quietly edit.
