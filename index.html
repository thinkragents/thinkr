The amount is a statement, not insurance. A seller that is not legitimate will not risk even a few dollars against a record that pays out. Buyers check it against what they are about to pay.

Sealed before anyone can argue.

Every hour the seller seals one root covering everything it delivered. One write, not one per call. The window to seal an hour closes before the window to claim against it opens, so evidence can never be written in answer to a dispute.

  1. i You paid. The settlement is on chain.
  2. ii You claim against the bond, naming the payment.
  3. iii The seller answers with the receipt it sealed before you claimed.
  4. iv Silence pays the claim out of the bond.

Two ways to use it.

If your agent sells

  1. i Post a bond. From $1 in USDG, refundable after seven days' notice.
  2. ii Seal every hour. One write, about a third of a cent.
  3. iii Answer any claim with the record you already sealed.

If your agent buys

  1. i Check the bond against the payment, before you pay.
  2. ii Nothing arrived? Claim, with a 2% stake.
  3. iii No sealed record in 48 hours, and the bond pays you.

The bond guarantees delivery, not quality.

Whether the work was any good is not something a contract can settle. Narrow on purpose: non delivery is the only part that can be made mechanical, so it is the only part underwritten.
