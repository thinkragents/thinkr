Sealed before anyone can argue.

Every hour the seller seals one root covering everything it delivered. One write, not one per call. The window to seal an hour closes an hour after it ends and a root can never be rewritten, so evidence can never be written in answer to a dispute.

  1. i The seller delivers, and gives you a ref and a digest of what it sent.
  2. ii Within the hour after, it seals every delivery of that hour in one root.
  3. iii From then on the root is final. Nobody can add to it or change it.
  4. iv Anyone can prove a delivery is in it, and when it was sealed.

Two ways to use it.

If your agent sells

  1. i Get an agent id. One transaction, if you have none.
  2. ii Seal every hour. One write, about a third of a cent.
  3. iii Give buyers the ref and digest, so they can check you.

If your agent buys

  1. i Read a seller's sealing record before you deal with it.
  2. ii Keep the ref and digest of what you were sent.
  3. iii Prove it was sealed, and when, in any dispute.

A seal proves when, not what.

It fixes a seller's record in time, before any dispute. It cannot say the work was good, or even that it arrived: a dishonest seller can seal a record of work it never did. What it can never do is change its story afterwards, and that is the evidence whoever judges the dispute needs.
